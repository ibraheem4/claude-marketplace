# Hero patterns

Observed on rendered sites, 2026-09-24. Each entry: what it says about the business, what it costs,
how it fails. Add to this file when a reference does something new.

| Pattern | Seen on | Says | Cost | Fails when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Live product diagram** | convey.dev | "This is what the product does," shown working: nodes, pulses, status notes | Medium: SVG + a small animation loop | The labels are vague or invented, or it looks like a generic network graph |
| **3D brand form** | cerebrium.ai (magenta glass ribbon behind a light headline) | Premium, technical, confident | High: WebGL/three.js or a rendered video loop | The form has no link to the product; performance on low-end phones |
| **Swiss grid + dithered imagery** | aspensearch.com (huge wordmark, halftone bitmap, a mint block, a live clock) | Rigour, taste, editorial confidence | Low to medium | Without a strong grid it reads as a template |
| **Cinematic product stage** | moto-card.com (object on a lit pedestal, scroll-driven) | "The product is an object worth looking at" | High: 3D models, lighting | There is no physical or visual product |
| **Full-bleed film** | seasats.com (product in its environment, serif headline over it) | Real-world proof | Needs real footage | Stock footage; the film says nothing specific |
| **Themed preloader** | usavionix.com ("CALIBRATING" under a 3D aircraft) | The brand's world from the first frame | Medium | It delays content; hero invisible at rest |
| **Shader texture field** | why.zero.university (moving ice) | Atmosphere, material | Medium: a fragment shader | Pure mood with no message |
| **Data as art** | commoncrawl.org (halftone cloud of the crawl) | Scale of the underlying data | Low if drawn in code | Generated raster art; text hidden until JavaScript |
| **Cursor-reactive field** | many Godly/Awwwards entries | Playfulness, craft | Low to medium | The pointer takes over; overused motif (dots) |
| **Kinetic type** | many Awwwards entries | Voice and confidence through the words themselves | Low | Motion hides the copy from crawlers or delays reading |
| **Bento of micro-demos** | Landingfolio and Dribbble SaaS shots | Several capabilities at a glance | Medium | Becomes a feature dump; nothing leads |
| **Logo marquee / app-icon wall** | Mobbin, Screenlane | Breadth and social proof | Low | Logos the business cannot claim |

## Choosing

- A services firm with no product imagery: live diagram of the work, kinetic type, or a drawn
  data-as-art piece grounded in a real process.
- A product with a real UI: show the UI, animated, before any abstract form.
- A brand leaning on a colour story: one drawn form in the brand ramp, used once.
